# Etsy listing — paste-ready

Etsy is the fastest route to a first sale because the buying intent already exists
there: people type "printable escape room" into Etsy. thekapture.com has to earn
that traffic; Etsy rents it. Fees are roughly 6.5% per sale plus $0.20 per listing,
so margin is worse. Take the trade knowingly — what Etsy buys is the answer to
whether parents want this, and the first reviews, which cannot be bought any other
way.

Sell the same kit at the same price. Do not discount to buy rank: a lower price
answers a different question from the one we need answered.

## Listing type

**Digital download.** Etsy delivers the files itself, instantly, with no external
host. Upload the same zip that is attached to the Wix product:
`release/The-Vanishing-Exhibit-v1.0.zip` (under Etsy's 20MB per-file limit — check
before upload; if it is over, upload the PDFs individually, up to five files).

## Title

Etsy weights the first 40 characters hardest, and matches on phrases rather than
single words. Lead with what people type.

```
Printable Escape Room Kit for Kids 9-12 | Museum Mystery Birthday Party Game | Instant Download | 25 Pages, 6 Puzzles, Host Guide
```

## Price

£22.00 (set the shop currency to GBP; Etsy converts for buyers). Same as the
direct listing. Never undercut your own store.

## Description

The first two lines are all that show before "read more" on mobile. They have to
carry the whole offer.

```
A printable escape room for 6–12 players aged 9–12. Print it, cut it, hand it out. One hour, six puzzles, no equipment beyond a printer and a pair of scissors.

WHAT YOU GET — instantly, on purchase
• 25 printable sheets, in A4 and US Letter
• Host guide (6 pages) written so you can run it without solving it first
• Player pack (8 pages) of evidence: a case file, coded cards, a floor plan, clock faces
• 24 hint cards, three levels per puzzle, so nobody is stuck for more than a minute
• Answer key
• Party extras: invitations, door signs, a recovery slip and a certificate

THE STORY
The Marlow Museum has lost its centrepiece exhibit overnight. The doors were locked
and the alarm never sounded. Your team has one hour with the case file.

WHY THIS AGE
Nine to twelve-year-olds reject anything that reads as babyish. Every sheet is
designed to look like a real document rather than a party game — typewriter faces,
filing tabs, rubber stamps, black on white. The puzzles are genuinely hard, and
each of the six rewards a different kind of thinking, so no one child solves the
whole party while the rest watch. Two puzzles are self-checking: two independent
routes reach the same number, so the team knows it is right without asking you.

PRINTING
Black and white, on ordinary paper. No full-page colour fills, nothing that eats
ink. A4 and US Letter versions are both included; use whichever your printer takes.

WHAT YOU NEED
A printer, scissors, an envelope, sticky tape, and pencils. Nothing else.

TRY IT FIRST
One complete puzzle, with its answer and its hint ladder, is free at thekapture.com.

THIS IS A DIGITAL PRODUCT
Nothing is posted to you. Files download immediately after purchase. Licensed for
personal, classroom, library and club use. Not for resale or redistribution.

PLAYTEST EDITION
This is version 1.0. Tell us what dragged and what landed and you get every later
version free.
```

## Photos — 10 slots, use at least 7

Etsy ranks listings with more photos higher, and the first image is the only one
most people see. Reuse the shop images already built.

| Slot | File | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `store/images/01-hero.jpg` | The one that appears in search. Do not change it. |
| 2 | `store/images/02-whats-inside.jpg` | Answers "how much do I get" |
| 3 | `store/images/03-the-puzzles.jpg` | Answers "is it any good" |
| 4 | `store/images/04-how-it-works.jpg` | Answers "can I actually run this" |
| 5 | `store/images/05-printing.jpg` | Kills the printing objection |
| 6 | `store/images/06-playtest-edition.jpg` | The feedback promise |
| 7 | `store/pins/pin-03-the-kit.jpg` | Vertical crop, fills the mobile slot |

**Never upload the answer key.** It prints the solution in a public listing.

## After the listing is live

1. Add the Etsy URL to `store/listing.md`.
2. Etsy's own stats tell you which of the 13 tags brought visits. Replace the
   bottom three every fortnight with variants of whatever is working.
3. Ask each buyer for feedback through Etsy Messages, once, after delivery. The
   first five reviews are worth more than the first five sales.
