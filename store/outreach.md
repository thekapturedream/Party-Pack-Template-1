# Outreach emails

Two templates. Send them one at a time, edited for the organisation. A mail merge
that shows is worse than no email.

Every send needs: the recipient's real name where the website gives one, one
specific detail about them, and the opt-out line kept intact.

Subject lines are lower case and specific on purpose. Title-case subjects with
exclamation marks read as bulk.

---

## 1. Schools and school libraries

**Subject:** a free printable escape room for year 5 and 6

```
Hello [name or "the library"],

I make printable escape-room kits for nine to twelve-year-olds, and I am giving
away one complete puzzle from the first one. It is free, there is nothing to sign
up to, and it prints in black and white on ordinary paper.

A4 and US Letter, with the answer and a three-level hint ladder so whoever runs it
does not need to solve it first:
https://www.thekapture.com/post/how-to-make-an-escape-room-at-home-the-six-puzzles-that-work-with-kids

It takes about eight minutes and works for a wet break or the last week of term.

If it lands, the full kit is six puzzles and twenty-five sheets, and the licence
covers classroom and library use — print it as often as you like, for as many
groups as you like, on one purchase.

I am building this properly rather than quickly, so if you do use it I would
genuinely like to know what dragged and what landed.

Rodney Manyepa
The Kapture · thekapture.com

If you would rather not hear from me again, reply with "no thanks" and I will
remove you.
```

## Tracking

Keep one row per organisation: name, type, address used, date sent, date followed
up, reply, opted out. A spreadsheet is enough. Under UK GDPR you must be able to
show what you sent and honour every opt-out, and the record also stops the second
round embarrassing you.

## The follow-up

One, a fortnight later, three sentences, no new offer:

```
Hello [name] — following up once on the free puzzle below, in case it was useful
for this term. If not, no reply needed and I will not write again.
```

Then stop. Reasoning and the legal position: `docs/lead-generation.md`.
