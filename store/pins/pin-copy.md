# Pinterest — pin copy and where each one points

Pinterest is the second channel because pins keep working for months, and party
planning is one of the things Pinterest is actually for. It is slow to start and
does not stop, which is the opposite of Etsy.

Three pins are built, 1000 × 1500, generated from real sheets by `scripts/pins.mjs`.
Regenerate them rather than editing them by hand.

**Every destination is a thekapture.com URL.** Nothing points at a third-party host.

## Boards to create first

Pinterest ranks a pin partly by the board it sits on, so the board name is part of
the keyword work. Create these three, in this order, and describe each one.

1. **Escape room parties for kids** — "Printable escape rooms, puzzle ideas and party plans for children aged 9 to 12."
2. **Birthday party ideas for 10 year olds** — "Indoor party ideas for the age that has outgrown soft play."
3. **Printable party games** — "Print-at-home games, puzzles and party paperwork."

## Cadence

Two to three pins a week, not thirty in one day. Pinterest suppresses bursts.
Re-pin the same three images to different boards with different descriptions
rather than making new art every week — the art is not the bottleneck, the
keyword coverage is.

## What to watch

Pinterest Analytics gives outbound clicks per pin. That number, not impressions,
is the one that matters. If a pin gets impressions and no clicks the description
is wrong; if it gets no impressions the board or the keywords are wrong.
