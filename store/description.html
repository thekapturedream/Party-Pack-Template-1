A museum mystery for one birthday afternoon. Six puzzles, one hour, and a host guide that means you never have to solve anything yourself.

The Clockwork Cartographer has vanished from Case 12 at the Marlow Museum. Four members of staff were in the building and every one of them says they saw nothing. Your Junior Curators have six pieces of evidence and until ten o'clock tomorrow morning.

Ages 9–12 · 3–12 players · 15 minutes to set up · 45–60 minutes to play

What happens at the party

Children work through six evidence sheets in order. Each one gives them a word. One letter from each word goes on the Recovery Slip, and the six letters spell where the automaton is hidden. You tape an envelope behind a mirror before they arrive. That is the whole setup.

The six puzzles

  • The Night Log — a sign-in sheet and a process of elimination
  • The Locker Note — a floor plan and a route to trace with a finger
  • The Curator's Cipher — a coded card and the museum's old shelving key
  • The Stopped Clock — two wrong clocks that agree on the right time
  • The Acquisitions Ledger — arithmetic that sends the team back to sheets they have already solved
  • The Backwards Note — mirror writing, read at a window or a mirror

No two puzzles use the same kind of thinking, so no single child runs away with the game. Two of them check themselves: the team knows it is right without asking you.

What you need

A printer, scissors, one envelope, sticky tape, pencils, and a mirror somewhere in the house. No props, no apps, no batteries. Black and white printing is fine throughout: nothing uses a full-page background and no puzzle needs colour to be solved.
