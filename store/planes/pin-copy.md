# Pinterest copy — Paper Plane Template Pack

Six pins in `store/planes/pins/`, 1000×1500, built from real pages of the pack
and from planes drawn by the fold engine wearing their actual liveries.

Pinterest is a search engine that happens to look like a mood board, so each pin
targets a query rather than a mood. Link every one to the product page unless
noted; the free plane pin links to the free sample.

---

**pin-01-ten-planes** → product page

> Ten paper planes that actually fly

Ten printable paper plane templates with the fold lines already on the sheet.
Distance, hang time, accuracy, and one that comes back to you. Every fold drawn
step by step. Ages 6–12, A4 and US Letter, instant download.

## Posting

Same rhythm as the escape room: one pin every two or three days rather than six
at once, each to a board it genuinely belongs on. Pinterest rewards steady
posting and punishes a burst. The free-plane pin goes first — it is the one with
a reason to click that costs the reader nothing.
