# Etsy listing — Paper Plane Template Pack, paste-ready

The shop-level work is already done for *The Vanishing Exhibit*: currency, Offsite
Ads, policies, shop title, the link to thekapture.com. See `store/etsy-listing.md`
for that; none of it needs repeating. This file is the second listing only.

**One thing to decide first.** The shop's advice was "do not open a second listing
until the first has data". That advice was about *cloning a listing that is not
converting*. This is a different product at a different price for a different
search term, which is the case the rule was not written for — a second listing
here doubles the shop's surface area in Etsy search rather than splitting it. Open
it, and keep the two listings' tags disjoint.

## Listing type

**Digital download.** Upload `release/Paper-Plane-Template-Pack-v1.0.zip`. It is

## Description

The first two lines are all most buyers see on a phone, so they carry the whole
pitch. The rest can be pasted from `store/planes/description.html` with the tags
stripped, or retyped from it.

```
Ten paper planes that actually fly — with the fold lines already printed on the
sheet you fold, and every fold drawn out step by step.

No glue, no scissors, no tape. Ordinary printer paper. Print at 100%, fold along
the printed lines, and the plane comes out right the first time.
```

Then the squadron list, the 34-sheet contents, and the printing notes, in that
order. Keep "A4 and US Letter" high: it is the single commonest pre-purchase
question on printable listings.

## Price

**£12.00**, matching the Wix price. Do not discount to buy rank — a lower Etsy
price answers a different question from the one worth answering, and it trains
buyers to wait for the cheaper channel.

At £12 the Etsy fee stack (transaction 6.5%, payment processing 4% + £0.20,
regulatory ~0.48%, listing, VAT on fees) comes to roughly **£2.10**, so about
**£9.90** nets per sale against roughly £11.65 selling direct. Check the current
numbers in your dashboard before relying on them.

## Images

The eight from `store/planes/images/`, in numbered order. Etsy shows the first at

clears Etsy's 2000px recommendation comfortably.

## The first week

- **Day 1:** publish, then look at the listing on a phone. First image, first two
  lines.
- **Day 1:** pin it to Pinterest from the Etsy page as well as from thekapture.com.
- **Days 2–7:** watch Etsy Stats for the search terms bringing visits. That is the
  same instrument as Search Console and it reports far faster.
- **After 14 days:** replace the three weakest tags with variants of whatever the
  stats show is working. Repeat fortnightly.
- **On the first sale:** message the buyer once, after delivery, and ask how the
  folding went. That question is worth more than the sale.

## Why this listing may outperform the escape room on Etsy

Worth knowing so the numbers are read correctly rather than as a verdict on the
first product. "Paper airplane" is a far higher-volume search than "printable
escape room", the price is half, and the buying decision is smaller — nobody has
to plan a party to justify it. Expect more sales and less revenue per sale. The
useful comparison is not units; it is which listing produces the first review.
